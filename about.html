About

A small team with big-team habits

Xitorque Solutions is a growing digital team based in Sukkur, Pakistan. We build apps and websites, run online stores, and make games and ads, all under one roof.

Our story

Xitorque began with a founder who taught himself design, then Python, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and then e-commerce. Today he leads a team of six that combines hands-on development experience with modern AI tools to ship quickly.

What we learned building our own apps, games and online store is exactly what we now bring to client projects: practical, tested and made for real businesses.

Two brands, one team

Xitorque Solutions is the agency. It builds websites, apps and Shopify stores, manages Daraz stores and automates the busywork.

Xitorque Studio is the creative division. It makes games and product ads.
