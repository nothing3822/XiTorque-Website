Questions

Quick answers

Who do you work with?

Shop owners, local businesses, online sellers and startups, in Pakistan and abroad. If you need something built or managed online, we can help.

Can I see examples before I decide?

Yes. The apps and games on the Products and Studio pages are live and can be opened or installed. For anything that is not public yet, such as our Shopify theme, ask and we will arrange a demo.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. Where a service shows a starting price, that is the minimum, and we always agree the final price with you before work begins.

How long does a project take?

A simple website can take a few days; a custom app takes longer. You get an honest estimate after we understand your idea.

Do the apps work on phones?

Yes. Everything we build is mobile-first, and our apps can be installed on the home screen like a normal app.
